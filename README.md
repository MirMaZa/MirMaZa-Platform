# MirMaZa Platform

## AI-Powered B2B & B2C Procurement & Supply Chain Platform

MirMaZa Platform is an AI-powered procurement ecosystem that connects manufacturers, suppliers, businesses, and consumers through a single digital platform.

The project aims to reduce procurement costs, eliminate unnecessary intermediaries, improve pricing transparency, and simplify supply chain management using modern digital technologies and Artificial Intelligence.

## Problem

Businesses often face:

- High procurement costs
- Multiple intermediaries
- Limited supplier transparency
- Manual procurement processes
- Difficult supplier comparison
- Lack of centralized purchasing solutions

---

## Solution

MirMaZa Platform will provide:

- AI-powered supplier recommendations
- Smart product search
- Supplier comparison
- Procurement automation
- Inventory monitoring
- Logistics integration
- Business analytics
- Secure digital transactions
